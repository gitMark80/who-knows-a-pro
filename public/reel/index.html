How to record a reel
  1. Tap Load site. Wait for whoknowsapro.com to appear in the frame.
  2. Tap Ready. The title card appears and everything else hides.
  3. Start your phone's screen recording from Control Center, then swipe back.
  4. Tap the title card. The category and city get picked for you and the results page loads.
  5. The site is live: scroll through the results and tap View profile on one if you like. (Set End card after to Manual if you want unhurried time.)
  6. The end card appears when time is up, or tap the Who Knows a Pro? badge to end whenever you're done. Stop recording.
  7. Post the video as a Reel. If Control Center shows in the first second, trim it in the Reels editor.
